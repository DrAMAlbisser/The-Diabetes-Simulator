
THE DIABETES SIMULATOR
       
       
       
       Introduction
       
       
       
            Just as a pilot can sit in an imaginary cockpit with a screen and
       control stick before him to navigate the skies, so can you sit in front
       of your DIABETES SIMULATOR and learn to manipulate the ever changing
       blood glucose values  of a person with diabetes.
       
            The challenge you face is to control  blood glucose with just the
       right amount of insulin.
       
            You will become an expert as you learn about the effects of
       different insulins on blood glucose values. With the DIABETES SIMULATOR
       you can compare your skills against the INSULIN DOSAGE COMPUTER (IDC)
       which serves as a handy reference.
       
       
       
       GOOD LUCK !
